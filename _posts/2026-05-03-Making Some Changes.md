## Making a change
One of my goals this year was to post on here more, so I wanted to just write an update regarding my thinking towards my career.

For most of my post college working life I always wanted to be in cybersecurity. I studied almost weekly on different things, setup a [inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/) newsfeed to keep up with new developments, and got some certifications as well. But it hasn't really panned out how I would have liked it. Also the current job market and increase in AI usage has seem to have made it much harder to get into entry level positions.

With all this happening I decided to make a bit of a change in what path I'm going to pursue. Maybe I should have done this earlier but I am going to try down the path of system administrator. I will keep posting on here with different things I am working on as well. I recently completed a small lab creating a domain controller, adding computers to it, and using chocolatey to push apps out to it. It was fun to create a new AD server, I haven't done that since college.

But yeah, here's to a new path and some new things to work on.

Brandon
